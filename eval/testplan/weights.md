# Scoring weights — #OneShotShop v2 acceptance test

> Generated by `calc/render.py`. The check counts are taken from `testplan.md`, so they always match it.

## Areas

Every check belongs to exactly one area, shown in square brackets after its ID. Each area has a fixed, published weight, and the weights add up to 100.

The hard B2B domain areas carry 68 points: pricing, deposits, fractional quantities, promotions, VAT, shipping rules, payments, inventory, catalog structures, B2B workflows, finance, international, workflows and fulfillment. Orders, returns, accounts, the back-office, storefront, UI quality, safety and reporting carry the remaining 32 points.

**Rationale for the final weights (after the evaluator audit of run 2):**
- **Back-office (ADM) is core.** It rises to 7 points, the largest single area.
- **Funding.** Pricing, deposits, fractional quantities, promotions and UI quality give up points. Their checks are mature and were passed by strong builds.
- **New capabilities.** Company roles and approvals, quotes, standing orders, e-invoicing, payment terms and dunning, payment edge cases, countries and currencies, order workflows, warehouses and drop-shipping get their own areas.
- **Cap per check.** No single check may weigh more than about 0.4 points (weight ÷ checks ≤ 0.41; the renderer enforces this), so one lucky or unlucky check cannot move the score much.
- **Scoring is stricter overall.** Checks now need positive evidence (positive controls for negative-only checks, quoted messages for rejections), and expected values are hidden from the evaluator (`testplan.evaluator.md`) and graded against `expectations.json`.

## Formula

For each area *a*: `score_a = passed_a / checks_a`, a number between 0 and 1.

```
total score = Σ_a  weight_a × score_a        (0 … 100, report with one decimal)
```

- Every check is PASS or FAIL. FAIL includes "not found", "blocked by …" and "reset failed".
- Within an area, all checks count equally.
- Also report the raw count `passed / 524` and the 22 area scores, so that shops can be compared area by area.
- No check is ever dropped from the denominator. A check that could not be executed is a FAIL.
